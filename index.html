I'm a researcher at Uppsala University funded by the Wenner-Gren Foundations. I'm an expert on Kalamang (Papuan) and Uruangnirin (Austronesian), the languages spoken on the Karas Islands in eastern Indonesia. I have also collected data on other Austronesian languages of eastern Indonesia: Geser-Gorom, Onim, and Yamdena. Until mid 2026, I'm leading a project called Mapping Bomberai, which aims to collect data on the understudied languages of that peninsula.

I support speakers of Wancho (a Tibeto-Burman language) to document their language and dialectal variation.

I collaborate with researchers from Google (among others) to understand how well large language models can read grammars and process field recordings. See this paper about grammar-reading (follow-up in this tweet) and this unpublished paper about grammar-reading and audio processing.

I have a side-project on Dutch multi-ethnolect as used in hip-hop lyrics. A database with 6,695 lyrics is available on OSF.

Previously, I worked on Scandinavian prosody in the ExSynOp project at UiT The Arctic University of Norway.

My PhD is from Lund University (Sweden), for which I wrote a grammar of Kalamang, a Papuan language of eastern Indonesia and a neighbor language of Uruangnirin. I also created a dictionary and an archive.

Mapping Bomberai (2024–2026)

In this project, I aim to collect data on the understudied languages of the Bomberai peninsula. It is financed by the Wenner-Gren Foundations with administrative support from Uppsala University and the Center for Endangered Languages Documentation in Manokwari, Indonesia.
